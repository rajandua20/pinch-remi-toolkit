FULL VOICEOVER (read as one continuous script, 166 words, ~60s at conversation pace)
===========================================================

Remi sets up a business's payments by conversation, and fixes them when they break. Built on Pinch.

Here's a swim school with sixty families and nothing set up. I type what I charge — fifty-nine a week for ten weeks, hundred-dollar deposit — and paste the family list. Remi shows me exactly what it will create. I approve. Sixty plans, sixty setup links, one sitting. The last platform that onboarded me took a week of forms to get here.

Two weeks later a debit bounces. I ask Remi what's wrong. It reads the dishonour code — insufficient funds, safe to retry — drafts the message to the parent, and waits. I approve, and it recovers. Nothing moves money without that yes. The approval gate sits in the server, not in the prompt.

Remi sets up payments by conversation, and fixes them when they break. I hate being made to spend a week onboarding before I can take one payment. Remi removes that. Powered by Pinch.

NOTES FOR THE GENERATOR
===========================================================
- Visual style: dark navy (#0B0F20) background, indigo accent (#4F46E5), light blue accent (#63B3ED), green for success (#34D399), red for failure (#F87171), white text, Poppins-style rounded sans-serif.
- Camera: static screen-capture framing throughout — no presenter on camera, no B-roll of people. Treat every shot as a laptop-screen recording with light cursor motion (click on Approve buttons) and a slow push-in (2–4% scale) per shot to keep it alive.
- Pacing: hold each shot exactly to its timecode; do not let text linger past its window. Lower-third overlays enter ~0.3s after the shot starts and exit ~0.3s before it ends.
- Audio: calm, confident, conversational — not a sales pitch read. The line "nothing moves money without that yes" should land as the clear emphasis point of the middle third.
- Transitions: hard cuts between shots, no wipes/spins. One exception: Shot 1 → Shot 2 and Shot 10 → Shot 11 can use a 0.2s crossfade.
- All screenshots referenced above are in /video/assets/ alongside this script.
